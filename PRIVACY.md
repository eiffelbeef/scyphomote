# Privacy Policy

Scyphomote respects your privacy.

## No Data Collection
Scyphomote does not collect, store, or process any of your personal data or usage information.

## Direct Communication
All communication occurs directly between your device and your Jellyfin server. No data is sent to the developer or any third parties.

## Local Storage
The app only stores necessary connection details (server address and authentication tokens) locally on your device to facilitate connection to your Jellyfin server.
